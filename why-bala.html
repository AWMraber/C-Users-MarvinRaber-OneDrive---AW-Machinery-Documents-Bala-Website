ABOUT BALA MAKINA

Precision, practical technology and industrial construction for productive panel processing.

At Bala Makina, we bring innovation and precision together in our vertical panel saws, offering efficient solutions for space-conscious workshops and factories. Designed for accuracy and durability, our machines deliver flawless vertical and horizontal cuts across a wide range of materials.

With decades of expertise in woodworking machinery, Bala Makina sets new standards in vertical panel saw technology. Where compact structure meets industrial-grade performance — this is vertical & horizontal cutting, redefined.

With more than 35 years of experience manufacturing vertical panel saws and thousands of machines installed worldwide, BALA has built its reputation on precision, durability, and continuous innovation. Decades of real-world experience have helped BALA continually refine its machines to meet the evolving demands of today’s woodworking industry.

BALA’s DPM ED, DPM KS, and DPM ED AV series combine proven construction with advanced technology and operator-focused features designed to improve accuracy, efficiency, and ease of use.

Every BALA vertical panel saw comes equipped with valuable features as standard, including integrated dust collection, dual horizontal measuring readouts for convenient repeat cuts, a digital vertical readout, and a repetitive cutting tool, along with additional features designed to make everyday operation easier and more productive.

At BALA, we believe the tools you need to get the job done right shouldn’t come with a long list of additional charges. That’s why our machines are built to deliver exceptional standard equipment, dependable performance, and long-term value from day one.

And with a 2-year parts warranty included standard, BALA stands behind the quality of every machine.
